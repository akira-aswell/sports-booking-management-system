# BR-001 — Identity and access

Status: Draft; business approval pending. Sprint: 1. Suggested owner: Tuấn.

## Business goal
Users need secure access without granting themselves privileged roles.

## In scope
Public registration, login, role checks and current-user identity.

## Out of scope
SSO, MFA and password recovery.

## Proposed success measure
The linked use cases satisfy their reviewed acceptance criteria on the applicable environment; record actual results in the QA log.
