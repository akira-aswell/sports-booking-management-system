# BR-005 — Demo payments and notifications

Status: Draft; business approval pending. Sprint: 2. Suggested owner: Tuấn.

## Business goal
Booking parties need transparent status updates and a way to demonstrate payment review.

## In scope
Manual reference submission/verification, refund-review marker, in-app notifications.

## Out of scope
Real money, bank APIs, automatic refunds, email and SMS.

## Proposed success measure
The linked use cases satisfy their reviewed acceptance criteria on the applicable environment; record actual results in the QA log.
