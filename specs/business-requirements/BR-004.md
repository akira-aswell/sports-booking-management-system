# BR-004 — Court reservations

Status: Draft; business approval pending. Sprint: 2. Suggested owner: Vũ / Hùng.

## Business goal
Players and owners need a reliable reservation workflow without double allocation.

## In scope
Create, list, approve, reject, cancel and reschedule, fee calculation and shared occupancy protection.

## Out of scope
Automatic expiry, attendance tracking and automatic completion.
