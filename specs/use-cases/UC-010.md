## Goal
Demonstrate payment review without transferring money.

## Preconditions
CONFIRMED booking; payment UNPAID/SUBMITTED for submission.

## Main flow
1. Player submits reference.
2. Payment becomes SUBMITTED; owner notified.
3. Owning owner confirms submitted reference; payment becomes PAID; notify player.
