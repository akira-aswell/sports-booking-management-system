## Goal
Create valid fixtures and assign compatible available courts.

## Preconditions
OPEN tournament with approved teams; IN_PROGRESS for scheduling.

## Main flow
1. Generate round robin pairs or first knockout round.
2. Transition tournament to IN_PROGRESS.
3. Assign court with matching sport, valid interval and tournament period.
4. Reject court conflicts and same-team simultaneous matches in same tournament.
