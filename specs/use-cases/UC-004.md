## Goal
Maintain facility information.

## Preconditions
Authenticated owner or admin; active sport.

## Main flow
1. Create court with metadata and pricing.
2. Backend assigns owner from authenticated identity and initializes weekly hours.
3. Owner updates own court or disables it; other owners denied.
