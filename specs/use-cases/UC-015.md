## Goal
Track tournament progress.

## Preconditions
Tournament IN_PROGRESS; match scheduled or finished (subject to knockout rules).

## Main flow
1. Record score, mark match FINISHED.
2. Round robin standings use points, goal difference, goals scored and name tie-break.
3. Knockout generates next round after all matches in current round finish; final completion closes tournament.
