## Goal
Inspect usable slots before requesting a booking.

## Preconditions
Active court and date.

## Main flow
1. Choose local date and duration.
2. Generate slots every 30 minutes inside opening hours.
3. Exclude elapsed time and maintenance; Sprint 2 also excludes active allocations and buffers.
4. Return slot flags and reasons; creation revalidates availability.
