## Goal
Manage entry into an amateur tournament.

## Preconditions
Active sport; authorized actor.

## Main flow
1. Create tournament OPEN.
2. Captain submits team while OPEN; capacity counts pending and approved.
3. Organizer reviews pending entries; duplicate team refused; notify captain.
