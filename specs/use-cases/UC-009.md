## Goal
Change an eligible reservation without losing data.

## Preconditions
Booking is PENDING or CONFIRMED; cancel before start; reschedule by player/admin with UNPAID payment.

## Main flow
1. Cancel: check authorization and future start, change to CANCELLED, release allocation and notify other party.
2. Submitted or paid payment becomes REFUND_REVIEW on cancellation.
3. Reschedule: lock booking and court, validate new slot excluding own allocation, recalculate amount, reset to PENDING and notify owner.
