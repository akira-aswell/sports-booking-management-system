## Goal
Inspect and review booking requests.

## Preconditions
Authenticated; correct ownership / scope.

## Main flow
1. List mine or owner scope; admin can list all.
2. Owner opens a PENDING booking and approves or rejects.
3. Approval confirms; rejection disables allocation; notify player.
